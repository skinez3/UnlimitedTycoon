## Key Features & Mechanics

### Base Building & Economy
* **Dynamic Claiming:** Start your journey by touching the entrance arch of any unoccupied base.
* **Passive Income:** Purchase and upgrade various automatic miners and servers to generate steady revenue.
* **Active Grinding:** Don't want to wait? Grab a manual miner and head to the mines to speed up your income generation.
* **Temporary Boosts:** Visit the in-game store to purchase temporary multipliers and upgrades to maximize your profits.

### Dynamic PvP & Safe Zones
The map is divided into distinct zones that alter player capabilities:
* **Safe Zone (Normal World):** Players are completely invulnerable. You can use the **Shift** key to sprint and navigate the map quickly.
* **PvP Zone:** Enter at your own risk! Invulnerability is disabled, and the ability to sprint is restricted, making combat tactical and high-stakes.

### RPG Progression
* **Weapon Research:** Unlock and research new types of weapons to dominate the PvP zones.
* **Stat Upgrades:** Improve your character's base characteristics to gain an edge over your rivals.
* **Stats UI:** Track your current power, health, and progression at any time via the dedicated "Stats" tab.

### Media Customization
Make your base truly yours by broadcasting your favorite content:
* **In-game Cinema & Garden Speakers:** Customize the visual and audio experience of your tycoon.
* **Easy Setup:** Click the gear icon to open settings and insert the **Roblox Asset ID** of the desired video or audio. (Find content via the [Roblox Creator Store](https://create.roblox.com/store)).

### Technical Details
* The game features a full save system for player progress.
* Scripts are scalable to accommodate new items, buildings, and features.
* The game features a full day/night cycle with changing sound effects.
